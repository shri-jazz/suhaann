SUHAANN PATEL — CRICKET PORTFOLIO (web version)

TO PUBLISH
1. Go to  https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag this whole folder onto the page.
3. Wait ~20 seconds. You get a URL like  https://random-name-123.netlify.app
4. Sign up free (Netlify prompts you) or the site expires in an hour.
5. Site settings > Change site name  ->  e.g. "suhaann-patel"
   Final URL: https://suhaann-patel.netlify.app

THEN (for link previews in WhatsApp / LinkedIn)
6. Double-click set-url.command, paste your live URL.
7. Drag the folder onto Netlify again to update.

WHAT'S HERE
  index.html          the page
  media/              photos + three match clips
  social-card.jpg     preview image used when the link is shared
  robots.txt          allows search engines to index the page

The three videos play in the browser on desktop and mobile.
